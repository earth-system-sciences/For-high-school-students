
This repository contains files to share and make high school students enthusiastic about Wageningen University education in general and Earth System Sciences in particular. Feel free to share!

- Flyers about the Earth System Sciences programmes in Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish and Portugese
- Flyers listing the study programmes related to soil, water and atmosphere (in Dutch)
